# Skier or IKEA-r?

A seven-question phone game: is the name on screen a cross-country ski racer or an IKEA product?

- `index.html` is the classic 7-question game; `sprint.html` is Sprint mode (qualification, quarterfinals, semifinals and a final against the World Cup sprint field, with lucky losers).
- `data.js` holds the shared name lists and World Cup standings.
- `docs/` is what GitHub Pages serves. Regenerate it with `./build.sh` after editing anything.

Skier names come from the FIS Cross-Country World Cup 2025/26 standings, FIS World Cup fields and the FIS registers of active Swedish and Norwegian racers. IKEA names come from IKEA Canada's series list, Lars Petrus's IKEA Dictionary (lar5.com/ikea) and namingschemes.com's IKEA list (product types). Just for fun; not affiliated with FIS or IKEA.
